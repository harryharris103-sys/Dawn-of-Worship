I haven't opened it in a browser, only checked that the script parses, so try it on your projector first.

**Stretching to fit the display**
- DISPLAY SETTINGS has a new **Media fit** option with three choices: Fill (crop to fit), Fit (show everything with black bars) and Stretch (distort to fill exactly).
- It applies to background images and videos, and to media projected on their own.
- A **Media fills whole display** checkbox makes backgrounds and media cover the entire output screen, even if the projector isn't 16:9. Text stays inside the normal 16:9 area.

**Projecting images or videos only**
- On the Media tab, a new dropdown next to Both/Preview/Live lets you pick **As background** or **Project only (full screen)**.
- In project-only mode, clicking an image or video shows it full screen on its own, with no text and no dimming, in Preview, Live or both. The "None" tile clears it.
- Videos have two new checkboxes, **Loop videos** and **Video sound (output)**, and Space plays or pauses the live video.
- Sound plays only on the output window, so you don't hear it twice. If the browser blocks sound at first, click the output window once.

Your own PowerPoint slides keep their original layout, and the existing text, Bible and song slides work as before.
